In Loving Memory of

Enzo – Boxador Dog Memorial Tribute

A loving tribute to Enzo, a cherished Boxador remembered with love by Brooks.

2009 - 2026

Shared by Brooks, OH

It feels like just yesterday that I brought you home. I adopted you on one of the first days I started hanging out with Jess after work, back when she was volunteering at the rescue. That day changed my life forever. You were the beginning of everything for us, helping to start the beautiful family we have today. You watched us grow and became the most gentle, loving companion to Evora, Miura, and Reventón. You truly loved every kid in our family.

For the runt of a boxer/lab mix who topped out at just 40 pounds, your spirit was larger than life. You were always ready for an adventure, whether we were out camping, going on hikes, or just letting you run loose and play on our Aberdeen mountaintop. I will never forget how much of a hardcore Bengals fan you were, sitting right next to me on the couch with your eyes glued to the TV on game days.

Thank you for a lifetime of unconditional love and memories. You will always be our best friend.
