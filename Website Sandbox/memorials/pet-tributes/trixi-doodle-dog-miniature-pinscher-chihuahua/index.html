In Loving Memory of

Trixi Doodle – Miniature Pinscher/Chihuahua Dog Memorial Tribute

A loving tribute to Trixi Doodle, a cherished Miniature Pinscher/Chihuahua remembered with love by Bethany.

2014 - 2026

Shared by Bethany, AL

Our Sweet, Sassy Doodle-

for 11 wonderful years you filled our lives with laughter, love, and your unforgettable personality.

Our chunky little queen—always ready to eat, nap, sunbathe, and chase lizards like it was your life’s mission. We lovingly teased you, dressed you up in outfits and costumes, and adored every bit of your perfectly round, adorable self.

You were deeply loved by your girls—Jocelyn, Annabelle, and Piper—their companion, confidant, and constant source of joy.

And to Keyser… you were the boss. Barking, nipping, and keeping him in line—yet loving him in your own feisty way.

You were more than a dog. You were family.

The best dog we have ever had.

You are forever in our hearts, forever missed, and forever loved.🐾
